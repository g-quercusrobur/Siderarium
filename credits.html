Sources of the tradition

The planetary hours follow the Chaldean order; the angels follow the Shem HaMephorash as given by the Golden Dawn tradition; the tarot attributions follow the Golden Dawn paths of the Tree of Life. These are public tradition; their arrangement and weighting in Siderarium are proprietary.
