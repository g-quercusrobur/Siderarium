Version of 21 November 2026 · Siderarium, the macrocosmic clock

Who is responsible

This site and the Siderarium app are published by Hippocrene Design Works LLC, which is the data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and comparable laws.

What the app does with your data

  • Nothing leaves your device. The app computes the sky in your browser. Your settings, your birth data (date, time and place) and your saved charts are kept in the browser's local storage on your device. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else.
  • No cookies. The site and the app set no cookies. Local storage is used only for the settings you choose, which is strictly necessary for the app to remember them; for this reason no consent banner is shown.
  • No analytics, no trackers, no third-party requests. The fonts are served with the app rather than from a font service.
  • Location. The app asks for your position only when you press the "here" button, so that the horizon of the dial can be your own; without it the dial uses the city of your device's time zone. The browser asks your permission first. The position is kept on the device only, rounded to about a kilometre, and is cleared by choosing a city instead. While "here" stays chosen and the browser's permission stands, the app reads the position again on later visits, so that the dial follows the device.

Sharing, backups and other people's data

  • Shared images are made on your device. The share and save buttons draw the image in your browser and hand it to your device's share sheet or downloads; nothing passes through us. The natal and synastry views print the chart's name, birth date and place shown on screen; the file's name and the short text sent with it carry the name and the date too. Check before you send.
  • Backup files stay with you. An exported backup is a file saved to your device. We never receive a copy. It holds the saved charts and the display settings; it does not hold your position.
  • Other people's birth data entered for a synastry or a saved chart is stored the same way as yours, in local storage on your device only.

Hosting

The site and the app are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses to operate the service and meet its legal obligations, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive these logs.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side; everything the app knows about you is on your device and under your control. If you have a question under the GDPR you can write to Hippocrene Design Works LLC at siderarium@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.
