Siderarium · tarot plates · sources and rights

Deck: the Rider tarot, first printing (the "Pam-A" cards), drawings by Pamela Colman Smith (1878–1951),
published by William Rider & Son, London, December 1909 (the files are dated 1910 on Commons).

Rights: public domain. In the United States because published before 1931; in the country of origin and
the European Union because the author died more than seventy years ago (Smith 1951; Waite, the
copyright holder named on the files, 1942). The files on Wikimedia Commons carry the Public Domain Mark 1.0
and the tags PD-old-80-expired and CC-PD-Mark; verified on the file pages of Wands08.jpg and
RWS_Tarot_08_Strength.jpg on 1 November 2026. The images are shown exactly as scanned, unaltered.

Scans: Wikimedia Commons, from Holly Voley's scans of a 1909 deck (file history), source given as
http://muzendo.jp/blog/. Each file's page is https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:<name>, for example
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wands08.jpg and https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RWS_Tarot_08_Strength.jpg

Files used (62): the 22 trumps RWS_Tarot_00_Fool.jpg … RWS_Tarot_21_World.jpg, and the small cards two to
ten of each suit, Wands02–Wands10, Cups02–Cups10, Swords02–Swords10, Pents02–Pents10 (the aces and the
courts are not used by the decans). Names as on Commons.

The titles of the small cards ("Lord of Swiftness" and the rest) are the Golden Dawn's Book T (1888 and
after), out of copyright. The attribution of trumps to signs, planets and elements is the Golden Dawn's.
